## Research Vision

My research aims to develop trustworthy and secure intelligent systems by combining Artificial Intelligence and Quantum Key Distribution. I am particularly interested in applying AI to healthcare while ensuring the privacy and security of sensitive data through quantum communication technologies.

---

## Artificial Intelligence for Healthcare

My research in this area focuses on developing machine learning methods for healthcare applications, particularly mental health prediction. My work includes time-series learning, explainable AI, and predictive modeling to support clinical decision-making.

Research topics include:

- Artificial Intelligence for Healthcare
- Mental Health Prediction
- Explainable AI
- Time-Series Machine Learning

---

## Satellite-Based Quantum Key Distribution

My research investigates secure communication through satellite-based Quantum Key Distribution (QKD). My current work focuses on routing, network optimization, and resource allocation in quantum communication networks.

Research topics include:

- Satellite-Based Quantum Key Distribution
- Quantum Communication Networks
- Network Optimization
- Secure Communication

---

## Future Research

My long-term goal is to integrate Artificial Intelligence with secure quantum communication technologies to develop trustworthy AI systems for healthcare and other data-sensitive applications. I also plan to involve undergraduate and graduate students in interdisciplinary research at the intersection of AI, cybersecurity, and quantum networking.
